SV Group Believes – Media Relations Stands Above the Rest!

In this world of information and interconnectivity, no business can even think about operating without deploying and following a vigorous media management strategy. Media relations is one of the fundamental parts in the field of Investors Relations. When we say media, it includes all four of its available types. These days, internet based media outlets are also growing in their effectiveness and reachability. The SV Group right from its inception has kept a greater focus on finding alternatives. Notably, it’s our Media Relations service only, which provides our clients with a chance of getting closer to the web-based financial blogs and financial news sources.

The Essence of Our Media Relations Strategy

Corporate level story telling have its own norms and etiquettes. In the first step we assist our clients in making appropriate choices and otherwise in the creation of complementary media content and stories, suitable for the general market sensitivity. Later, these stories are routed for publication and sharing through all the possible slots. We do have a greater focus on the acquisition of interview opportunities at reputable financial media outlets

SV Group & Top-tier journalists have some mutual interests

We don’t hesitate while talking about our strong relationships with the top-tier financial and business journalists working around. Yes! For us, they are like a medium through which we used to share stories from our clients. However, we have made it a matter of mutual interest.  For our partner journalists and newsman, we are acting as a source of credible information. What they need is great and sellable stories. We used to work with our clients for crafting their stories in a media friendly tone. Believe us – it works better when mutual interests and stakes get involve.

SV Group is capitalizing all four types of media

We believe each type of media is interconnected with all of its other types. Thus, we cannot take anything for granted here. As an example consider online media that is operating with a single click based optional integration with the social media. Whether it’s owned, social, hybrid or traditional media – SV Group is not interested in overlooking any one of them.

The Creation of Selling Media

Seeding of more information in less time is the requirement of today’s world. A few seconds interaction will decide whether the prospective client is going to invest some of his or her time in reading your story or not. So, you have to think out of the box. You have to proceed with sellable ideas and engaging content. Consider a well-crafted post released on social media giant, Facebook. There, any such piece can get viral easily without an external intervention and efforts. We the SV Group are working with having a team of the most seasoned journalists onboard. Our clients will also enjoy the benefits of a training program offered along with our core media relations services.
